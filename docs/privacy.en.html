Privacy policy — FullTube

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Purpose

FullTube — Clean Video Window & Theater Mode is a Chrome and Edge extension that detects a visible HTML5 player on the page and opens the same page in a clean window. On YouTube, it also offers a theater mode in the same tab.

Data accessed

The extension can access the active page, the HTML5 video element and the technical playback state, such as current time, volume, mute state and whether it is playing. This access happens in your browser and is used only to find the video and apply the layout and playback state.

The extension asks for access to http and https sites because it cannot know in advance which site you will be on when you click the icon. The access is used only to find the video and apply time, volume and layout; no other page content is read, stored or sent.

Window size and position are stored locally, per site. Temporary data used to link the source tab to the window is kept in storage.session and removed when it is no longer needed.

Welcome page and language

On first install, the extension opens a welcome page once. It is a local page of the extension itself, loads no content from external servers and sends no data. The interface language (Portuguese or English) follows your browser language; this choice is made locally and no information is sent.

What we do not do

  • We do not read, copy or transmit cookies.
  • We do not access passwords, tokens or credentials.
  • We do not extract media URLs or download streams.
  • We do not use tab capture or stream capture (tabCapture, captureStream).
  • We do not block ads.
  • We do not send data to our own or third-party servers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Sharing and retention

No data is shared with third parties. Window preferences stay in the browser's local storage and can be removed by uninstalling the extension. Temporary session state is discarded when the browser session ends or when the window is closed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and the history will be available in the project repository.
